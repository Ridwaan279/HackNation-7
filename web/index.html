Traditional, or always on?

Both turn what you do into a step-by-step guide. The web app is a recording session. The desktop app keeps learning after the recording ends.

Online · traditional onboarding

Web app

You record a task. Protégé asks when something needs explaining, then sums it up and asks what's missing.

  • Records your screen and your voice
  • Asks questions out loud while you record
  • A screenshot every time the screen changes
  • At the end: an overview and a few last questions
  • Only learns while you record, and only sees pixels

Desktop app

Onboarding that never stops. It runs all the time and keeps training itself on the real work.

  • Everything the web app does, plus:
  • Runs all the time, not just while you record

The moonshot

We pushed this idea to its limit.

Traditional onboarding captures what an expert remembers to say on the day. Protégé keeps watching the real work, so it learns the exceptions, the shortcuts and the rules nobody thought to mention, and passes them on.

That was only possible with the desktop app. A browser sees pixels; the app sees the work.

What it does

It sees what a browser can't.

Teach

It sits beside your new hire.

The ghost watches their screen, flies to the right field and stops a mistake before it's saved, explained in the expert's own words.

Before you postEquipment over €5,000 goes to 0400, not 6100. That's how the expert does it.
Capture

Reads every field

Field names, typed values and button names, through Windows accessibility. Every click and key becomes a step.

Privacy

Keeps private things private

Password fields are never read. Cards, IBANs and keys are masked on your computer before anything is stored.

Voice

Asks why, out loud

An ElevenLabs voice listens while you work and asks one short question at each natural pause.

Map

Builds a Work Map

The decisions and rules behind the steps, in the expert's own words, confirmed by them.

Always on

It keeps learning after the recording ends.

In the background, it learns every app your team uses, as masked text only, and writes an App Profile for each. It picks up the exceptions nobody recorded, like a held invoice or a new supplier.

  • Learns while you work, 24/7
  • Skips password managers, banking and private windows
  • Pause it, or go off the record with Ctrl+Shift+O
