How onboarding works

One recording is a start. Always on is a teammate.

A recording captures the task you showed on the day you showed it. The desktop app keeps learning in the background, so the guide covers the exceptions too.

One recording Web and desktop

  • Day 1 The expert records the task once and explains it.
  • Day 1 The guide is ready: screenshots, steps and the expert's words.
  • Week 1 The new hire follows the guide on their own.
  • Later Exceptions nobody showed (a held invoice, a new supplier) still need the expert.

Always on Desktop app

  • Every day It learns each app the team uses, as masked text only, and builds an App Profile.
  • While recording It reads field names and values, and asks why at natural pauses, by voice.
  • After recording It drafts a Work Map of decisions and rules, and the expert confirms it.
  • Week 1 A ghost sits beside the new hire, points at the right field and stops a mistake before it's saved.

    Desktop app for Windows

    Everything the web recorder does, plus the parts a browser can't see.

    Browsers only let a page see the pixels of the screen you share. The desktop app reads the screen through Windows accessibility, so it knows which field you changed and what you typed, without storing passwords, cards or IBANs.
